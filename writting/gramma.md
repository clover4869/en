## 1. PEEL — template cho body paragraph

Dùng cho **mọi** body paragraph.

| Bước | Nội dung | Số câu |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| **P** — Point | Câu chủ đề: nêu điểm khác biệt (dùng mẫu câu Week 17) | 1 |
| **E** — Explain | Giải thích A thế nào, B thế nào | 2 |
| **E** — Evidence | Ví dụ, số liệu, nguồn, hoặc lý lẽ cụ thể | 2 |
| **L** — Link | Chốt lại, nối về thesis | 1 |

## 2. Checklist tìm ý khi bí

### 🔹 Two choices

| Góc nhìn | Câu hỏi gợi ý |
| --- | --- |
| 💰 **Cost** | Which one costs more? Who pays? Is it affordable for most people? Are there hidden costs? |
| 🚪 **Accessibility** | Can anyone use/do it? What do you need before starting? Is it available everywhere? Is it easy to access? |
| 🔄 **Flexibility** | Can you adapt it to your lifestyle? Can you change the schedule/method? What happens if your situation changes? |
| 🎯 **Effectiveness** | Which one works better? Which gives better results? How could you prove that? How consistent are the results? |
| 🧠 **Skills** | What must you know first? Is it difficult to learn? Does it require special skills? Does it require skills outside your main field? |
| 👥 **Social** | Do you do it alone or with others? Who do you meet? Do you build relationships? Is interaction planned or accidental? |
| ⚠️ **Risk** | What can go wrong? How serious is the problem? Who suffers the loss? How easy is it to recover? |
| ⏳ **Long vs. short term** | Which gives faster results? Which is better after 5–10 years? Are there short-term disadvantages but long-term benefits? |
| 👤 **Who it suits** | Who would choose this? Who would avoid it? Is it better for beginners or experienced people? Does age, personality, or situation matter? |

### 🔹 Two places / groups

| Góc nhìn | Câu hỏi gợi ý |
| --- | --- |
| 👤 **Who** | Who lives/works there? Who has power? Who benefits? Who is excluded? What kind of people are attracted to it? |
| 📍 **Where** | Where is it located? What is the environment like? Is it urban or rural? Is it easy to reach? What is nearby? |
| ⏰ **When** | When did it develop? When is it busiest? How has it changed over time? Does the time period affect it? |
| 👥 **People** | How do people interact? Do they know each other? Do they cooperate or compete? Are relationships formal or informal? |
| 🎯 **Purpose** | What is the place/group mainly for? What are they trying to achieve? What makes them different? |

### 🔹 Abstract topics

| Góc nhìn | Câu hỏi gợi ý |
| --- | --- |
| 🎯 **Purpose** | What is the purpose of each? What problem does it try to solve? Are they trying to achieve the same thing? |
| 📍 **Where / When** | Where does each exist or apply? When did it emerge? In what situation is it relevant? |
| 👤 **Who** | Who created it? Who controls it? Who uses it? Who benefits from it? |
| 🔧 **Requirements** | What does each require? What knowledge or resources does it need? Is it difficult to understand or apply? |
| 🎁 **Benefits** | What does each give people? What problem does it solve? What are its main advantages? |
| ⚠️ **Problems** | What can go wrong? What are its weaknesses? Who might be negatively affected? |
| 📈 **Change** | How has it changed over time? What did it look like 20 years ago? Is it becoming more or less important? |
| 🌍 **Impact** | How does it affect society? Does it affect individuals, groups, or the wider world? |
| ❓ **Importance** | If both disappeared tomorrow, which loss would be worse? Why? |

### 🔹 Nervous / no ideas

Bí quá thì chạy 5 cái này — luôn ra ý.

| Góc nhìn | Câu hỏi gợi ý |
| --- | --- |
| 💰 **Money** | Who pays? How much? Which is cheaper? Is it worth the cost? |
| ⏰ **Time** | How long does it take? Which is faster? Which is more flexible? Does it save time? |
| 👥 **People** | Alone or together? Who do you interact with? Does it help you meet people? |
| 🧠 **Skill** | What must you know first? Is it easy to learn? Does it require special training? |
| ⚠️ **Risk** | What can go wrong? How serious is it? Who suffers? How difficult is it to recover? |

## 3. Linking words & dấu câu

### 🟦 Contrast

| Word / Phrase | Cách dùng | Dấu câu / Lưu ý |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **whereas** | `Clause, whereas clause` | `,` trước **whereas** |
| **while** | `Clause, while clause` | `,` trước **while** khi mang nghĩa contrast |
| **although** | `Although clause, clause` | `,` sau mệnh đề **although** nếu đứng đầu |
| **even though** | `Even though clause, clause` | Giống **although** |
| **however** | `. However, clause` | Thường đầu câu mới + `,` |
| **nevertheless** | `. Nevertheless, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |
| **nonetheless** | `. Nonetheless, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |
| **in contrast** | `. In contrast, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |
| **by contrast** | `. By contrast, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |
| **on the other hand** | `. On the other hand, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |

### 🟦 Concession

| Word / Phrase | Cách dùng | Dấu câu / Lưu ý |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **although** | `Although + clause, clause` | Không dùng **although … but** |
| **even though** | `Even though + clause, clause` | Mạnh hơn **although** |
| **despite** | `Despite + noun/V-ing` | ❌ Không + full clause |
| **in spite of** | `In spite of + noun/V-ing` | ❌ Không + full clause |
| **despite the fact that** | `Despite the fact that + clause` | Dùng được với full clause |

### 🟦 Reason

| Word / Phrase | Cách dùng | Dấu câu / Lưu ý |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **because** | `because + clause` | + full clause |
| **since** | `Since + clause, clause` | Có thể mang nghĩa "kể từ khi" |
| **as** | `As + clause, clause` | Có thể chỉ nguyên nhân |
| **because of** | `because of + noun/V-ing` | ❌ Không + full clause |
| **due to** | `due to + noun` | Formal |
| **owing to** | `owing to + noun` | Formal |

### 🟦 Result

| Word / Phrase | Cách dùng | Dấu câu / Lưu ý |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **therefore** | `. Therefore, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |
| **thus** | `. Thus, clause` | Formal, thường đầu câu |
| **consequently** | `. Consequently, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |
| **as a result** | `. As a result, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |
| **as a consequence** | `. As a consequence, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |

### 🟦 Addition

| Word / Phrase | Cách dùng | Dấu câu / Lưu ý |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **moreover** | `. Moreover, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |
| **furthermore** | `. Furthermore, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |
| **in addition** | `. In addition, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |
| **additionally** | `. Additionally, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |
| **also** | `Subject + also + verb` | Thường **không cần comma** |

### 🟦 Example

| Word / Phrase | Cách dùng | Dấu câu / Lưu ý |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **for example** | `. For example, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |
| **for instance** | `. For instance, clause` | Giống **for example** |
| **such as** | `noun + such as + examples` | Không dùng như câu độc lập |
| **including** | `noun + including + examples` | + noun/noun phrase |

### 🟦 Similarity

| Word / Phrase | Cách dùng | Dấu câu / Lưu ý |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **similarly** | `. Similarly, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |
| **likewise** | `. Likewise, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |
| **in the same way** | `. In the same way, clause` | Thường đầu câu + `,` |

### 🟦 Sequence

| Word / Phrase | Cách dùng | Dấu câu / Lưu ý |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **firstly** | `Firstly, clause` | Đầu câu + `,` |
| **first of all** | `First of all, clause` | Đầu câu + `,` |
| **secondly** | `Secondly, clause` | Đầu câu + `,` |
| **finally** | `Finally, clause` | Đầu câu + `,` |
| **lastly** | `Lastly, clause` | Đầu câu + `,` |

### 🟦 Conclusion / Overview

| Word / Phrase | Cách dùng | Dấu câu / Lưu ý |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **in conclusion** | `In conclusion, clause` | Đầu câu + `,` |
| **to conclude** | `To conclude, clause` | Đầu câu + `,` |
| **overall** | `Overall, clause` | Rất hay dùng trong Task 1 |
| **in general** | `In general, clause` | Đầu câu + `,` |
| **in summary** | `In summary, clause` | Đầu câu + `,` |

### ⚠️ Lỗi dấu câu hay mất điểm nhất: comma splice

**however, therefore, moreover, in contrast…** KHÔNG nối được hai mệnh đề bằng dấu phẩy.

| | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- |
| ❌ Sai | Online courses are cheap**, however,** they lack interaction. |
| ✅ Đúng (dấu chấm) | Online courses are cheap**. However,** they lack interaction. |
| ✅ Đúng (chấm phẩy) | Online courses are cheap**; however,** they lack interaction. |
| ✅ Đúng (dùng liên từ) | Online courses are cheap**, but** they lack interaction. |

> **Mẹo nhớ:** `however` là **trạng từ**, không phải liên từ. Muốn nối câu bằng dấu phẩy thì phải dùng `and / but / or / so / yet` (FANBOYS).

## 4. Cấu trúc so sánh

### So sánh hơn kém

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- |
| `as + adj + as` | Online courses are **as effective as** classroom lessons. |
| `not as / so + adj + as` | Rural life is **not as fast-paced as** city life. |
| `far / much / significantly + comparative` | Online shopping is **far cheaper than** in-store shopping. |
| `slightly / marginally + comparative` | Private schools are **slightly more expensive than** public ones. |
| `twice / three times as + adj + as` | The city is **twice as crowded as** the countryside. |
| `by far the + superlative` | Cost is **by far the most important** factor. |
| `The + comparative…, the + comparative…` | **The longer** the course lasts, **the more** it costs. |

### Mẫu câu contrast

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- |
| `Unlike A, B…` | **Unlike** online shops, physical stores close at night. |
| `A differs from B in that + clause` | Online retail **differs from** traditional retail **in that** it has no rent costs. |
| `What distinguishes A from B is + noun` | **What distinguishes** online study **from** classroom study **is** flexibility. |
| `Whereas A…, B…` | **Whereas** cities offer jobs, **rural areas** offer space. |
| `A…, whereas B…` | Cities offer jobs, **whereas** rural areas offer space. |

### Mẫu câu similarity

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ | Lưu ý |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Both A and B + verb (số nhiều)` | **Both** online **and** classroom learning **require** discipline. | luôn chia số nhiều |
| `Neither A nor B + verb` | **Neither** method **is** perfect. | chia theo chủ ngữ **gần nhất** |
| `Like A, B…` | **Like** city dwellers, rural residents value convenience. | |
| `A…, and the same is true of B` | Cities are expensive, **and the same is true of** tourist towns. | |

### ❌ Lỗi so sánh kinh điển

| ❌ Sai | ✅ Đúng |
| --- | --- |
| more cheaper / more better | cheaper / better |
| different **with** | different **from** |
| compare **to** *(khi phân tích khác biệt)* | compare **with** |
| Cities are more expensive **then** villages. | more expensive **than** |
| the most **cheapest** | the cheapest |

## 5. Hedging — nói có mức độ

Giám khảo trừ điểm khi khẳng định tuyệt đối. Đừng viết `All people prefer…` — không chứng minh được.

| Thay vì | Viết là |
| --- | --- |
| All people prefer X | **Many people tend to prefer** X |
| X is always better | X **is generally** better / X **is often** better |
| X causes Y | X **may contribute to** Y / X **is likely to cause** Y |
| Everyone knows that… | **It is widely believed that…** |
| X will happen | X **is likely to** happen |

**Từ hedging hay dùng:** `tend to` · `generally` · `in most cases` · `are likely to` · `may / might` · `appear to` · `arguably` · `a significant proportion of` · `relatively`

> ⚠️ Đừng lạm dụng. Một câu **một** hedge là đủ. `X may possibly tend to perhaps…` là sai.

## 6. Lỗi người Việt hay mắc

| ❌ Sai | ✅ Đúng | Vì sao |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Although** it is expensive, **but** many people buy it. | **Although** it is expensive, many people buy it. | Tiếng Việt "Mặc dù… nhưng…" — tiếng Anh chỉ dùng **một** |
| **Despite** it is expensive… | **Despite the** high price… / **Despite the fact that** it is expensive… | `despite` + **noun**, không + clause |
| **Because of** it rains, … | **Because** it rains, … | `because of` + noun |
| I have many **informations**. | much **information** / many **pieces of information** | uncountable |
| The **studys** show… | The **studies** show… | -y → -ies |
| Government **should to** invest… | Government **should** invest… | modal + V nguyên thể |
| He enjoy psychological **beneficial**. | He enjoys psychological **benefits**. | sai word form *(đúng lỗi ở WEEK 17 Paragraph 3)* |
| Many people **makes** friends. | Many people **make** friends. | subject–verb agreement |
| **In my opinion, I believe** that… | **In my opinion,** … *hoặc* **I believe** that… | thừa *(WEEK 17 Wordiness)* |

### Danh từ không đếm được — không bao giờ thêm `-s`

`information` · `advice` · `knowledge` · `research` · `equipment` · `furniture` · `progress` · `evidence` · `feedback` · `homework` · `traffic` · `money`

Muốn đếm thì: **a piece of** information, **two pieces of** advice.

### Mạo từ (a / an / the) — quy tắc nhanh

| Tình huống | Dùng | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nhắc lần đầu, đếm được, số ít | `a / an` | She opened **a** shop. |
| Nhắc lại cái đã biết | `the` | **The** shop is downtown. |
| Nói chung, số nhiều | *(không mạo từ)* | **Cities** are crowded. |
| Danh từ duy nhất | `the` | **the** government, **the** environment |
| So sánh nhất | `the` | **the** most important factor |

## 7. Register — văn viết học thuật

| ❌ Nói / informal | ✅ Viết / academic |
| --- | --- |
| don't, can't, isn't | do not, cannot, is not |
| **you** should save money | **people** should save money / **one** should |
| a lot of / lots of | many, a great deal of, a significant number of |
| big / huge | significant, substantial, considerable |
| things / stuff | factors, aspects, elements |
| get | obtain, receive, become |
| kids | children |
| find out | discover |
| put off | postpone |
| ... and so on / etc. | *(bỏ hẳn — liệt kê xong thì dừng)* |
| I think / I guess | It can be argued that… / This suggests that… |

> **Bài contrast thuần (template 1 trong WEEK 17) không được nêu ý kiến cá nhân.** Muốn nêu ý kiến thì dùng template 2 (*ESSAY WITH CONTRAST AND PERSONAL OPINION*).

## 8. Quy tắc đoạn văn

**Quy tắc gọn để nhớ**

1. Câu cuối đoạn **không được** là ví dụ.
2. Câu cuối đoạn **không được** lặp câu đầu.
3. Ngoài hai điều đó, 4 câu hay 6 câu tuỳ ý.

**Thêm mấy điều nữa**

4. Mỗi đoạn **một ý chính** thôi. Hai ý → tách hai đoạn.
5. **Topic sentence đứng đầu đoạn**, không giấu ở giữa.
6. Không bắt đầu đoạn bằng `And` / `But` / `Because`.
7. Tránh `this` trơ trọi — viết `this trend`, `this difference`, `this approach`. Giám khảo cần biết `this` chỉ cái gì *(WEEK 17 gọi là **unclear pronoun reference**)*.
8. Mỗi body paragraph nên nói về **cả A và B**, không phải đoạn 1 chỉ nói A, đoạn 2 chỉ nói B.

## 9. Checklist trước khi nộp

- [ ] Thesis có nêu **rõ 2–3 điểm** sẽ so sánh chưa?
- [ ] Mỗi body paragraph có đủ **PEEL** chưa?
- [ ] Mỗi body paragraph có nhắc **cả A và B** chưa?
- [ ] `however / therefore / in contrast` có bị nối bằng **dấu phẩy** không? → sửa thành `.` hoặc `;`
- [ ] Có `although … but` không? → bỏ `but`
- [ ] `despite` / `because of` có bị theo sau bởi **full clause** không?
- [ ] Chủ ngữ số nhiều — động từ đã chia đúng chưa?
- [ ] Có **contraction** (`don't`, `isn't`) không? → viết đầy đủ
- [ ] Có dùng `you` không? → đổi sang `people`
- [ ] Có câu khẳng định tuyệt đối (`all`, `always`, `never`) không? → thêm hedge
- [ ] Có cụm **wordy / redundant** nào không? → xem bảng WEEK 17
- [ ] Kết bài có **closing statement** (prediction / recommendation) chưa?
- [ ] Đủ **350–400 từ** chưa?
