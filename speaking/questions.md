# EXAM PREPARATION FOR ENGLISH SPEAKING SKILL

## 1. UNIT 1: SHOPPING

### PART 1

- Do you like buying shoes? How often?
- Have you ever bought shoes online?
- How much money do you usually spend on shoes?
- Which do you prefer, fashionable shoes or comfortable shoes?

### PART 2

> **SPEAKING CARD 1**
>
> **Describe your favorite shop that you often visit.**
>
> *You should say*
> - What products or services it offers
> - Where it is located
> - What the shop looks like
>
> *and explain why you enjoy shopping there.*

### PART 3

- Is shopping a popular activity in your country
- How have shopping habits changed over recent years?
- To what extent do you think advertising affects the way people shop?
- Do you think shopping habits are likely to change in the future?
- Do all people enjoy shopping for clothes?

## 2. UNIT 2

### PART 1

- Are you a patient person?
- What is it that makes you feel impatient?
- How do you feel when you have to do something for a long time?
- Does your job require you to be patient?
- Are you more patient now than when you were a child?

### PART 2

> **SPEAKING CARD 2**
>
> **Describe a person you know whose personality matches his/her career very well.**
>
> *You should say*
> - Who the person is
> - What his/her job is
> - What kind of personality the person has
>
> *and explain how this personality contributes to success in the job.*

### PART 3

- Do you think it is better for children to have a few close friends or many casual friends?
- Do you think a child's relationship with friends can be replaced by that with other people, like parents or other family members?
- What are the differences between friends made inside and outside the workplace?
- Do you think it's possible for bosses and their employees to become friends?

## 3. UNIT 3

### PART 1

- Would you prefer an exciting holiday or a peaceful holiday?
- Where did you go for your last holiday?
- Would you recommend your city to others?
- What's the weather like where you live?
- Are there people of different ages living in this city?
- Are the people friendly in the city?

### PART 2

> **SPEAKING CARD 3**
>
> **Describe a city or a country you most want to live in the future.**
>
> *You should say*
> - What city/ country it is
> - Where it is located
> - What features it has
>
> *and explain why you would like to live in this city/ country.*

### PART 3

- How does technology affect the way people spend their leisure time?
- Do you think only old people have time for leisure?
- Why do old people prefer to live in quiet places?
- Why are there more noises made at home now than in the past?
- Will you go to a foreign country to travel because of the distinct landscape?
- How can tourists have a good travel experience?
- What kinds of comments do travelers typically leave online after a trip?

## 4. UNIT 4

### PART 1

- Do you love flowers?
- Do you know anyone who loves flowers?
- Are there a lot of flowers where you live?
- Do you take photos of flowers in your daily life?
- Do people in your country often send flowers to others?

### PART 2

> **SPEAKING CARD 4**
>
> **Describe a place of natural beauty in the world that you would like to visit.**
>
> *You should say*
> - Where it is located
> - What its natural features are
> - What activities you could do there
>
> *and explain why it's an interesting place to visit.*

### PART 3

- Is it easy to find quiet places in your country? Why?
- How do people spend their leisure time in your country?
- What's the difference between the city and the countryside?
- Some people say large cities are suitable for old people. What do you think?
- Do you think it is possible that all of the population move to cities?
- Do you think people in the countryside are friendlier than people in the city?
- Are there any changes in your city?

## 5. UNIT 5

### PART 1

- Do you know many small businesses where you live?
- Do you prefer buying things from big companies or small businesses?
- Have you ever worked in small businesses?
- Have you ever thought about starting your own business?

### PART 2

> **SPEAKING CARD 5**
>
> **Describe a business leader you admire.**
>
> *You should say*
> - Who the person is
> - What kind of business he/she leads
> - What makes him/her a good leader
>
> *and explain how he/she inspired you.*

### PART 3

- Why do some people choose to start their own company?
- What are the advantages and disadvantages of family businesses?
- What family businesses do you know in your local area?
- What makes a successful family business?

## 6. UNIT 6

### PART 1

- What is your favourite machine in your home?
- Do you think washing machines and sweeping machines are important?
- Would you like to use a computer or tablet?
- In what conditions would you use a computer or tablet?
- When was the first time you used a computer or tablet?

### PART 2

> **SPEAKING CARD 6**
>
> **Describe a well-designed object that you use regularly.**
>
> *You should say*
> - What the object is
> - What you use it for
> - What design features you like
>
> *and explain its design contributes to its value or usability.*

### PART 3

- Do you think clothes produced in the factory are of better quality than those made by hand?
- Do you think handmade clothes are more valuable?
- Is the older generation better at fixing things?
- How has consumer culture changed the way people value objects?
- Why do some people prefer to keep old items instead of buying new ones?
- Are expensive objects always more meaningful than cheap ones?

## 7. UNIT 7

### PART 1

- Do you think your hometown has a clean environment? Why or why not?
- Do you do anything in your daily life to protect the environment?
- How do you usually commute? Do you think it's environmentally friendly?
- Do you prefer spending time indoors or outdoors? Why?
- Do you bring reusable bags or bottles when you go out?
- Has the environment in your hometown changed in the last few years? How?

### PART 2

> **SPEAKING CARD 7**
>
> **Describe a serious environmental problem in your country.**
>
> *You should say*
> - What it is
> - What causes this problem
> - What effects it has on people or nature
>
> *and explain what can be done to solve this problem.*

### PART 3

- Why should we protect wild animals?
- Why are some people more willing to protect wild animals than others?
- Do you think it's important to take children to the zoo to see animals?
- What problems will occur if people don't recycle?
- Should the government encourage people to recycle items?
- How do people in your country recycle various items they don't want?
- Is it more important to protect wild animals or the environment?

## 8. UNIT 8

### PART 1

- How do you usually keep yourself healthy?
- Do you think you have a healthy lifestyle? Why or why not?
- What kinds of food do you try to eat to stay healthy?
- How often do you exercise?

### PART 2

> **SPEAKING CARD 8**
>
> **Describe a time when you have a positive experience with a medical professional such as a doctor, a nurse or dentist.**
>
> *You should say*
> - Who the medical professional was
> - When and where the experience happened
> - Why you had to see him/her
>
> *and explain why it was a positive experience for you.*

### PART 3

- Should healthcare be free for everyone? Why or why not?
- How has technology changed the way people take care of their health?
- Do you think mental health is as important as physical health? Why?
- What can communities do to help people live healthier lives?
- Do you think advertising influences people's health choices?

## 9. UNIT 9

### PART 1

- Are you interested in history? Why or why not?
- Did you learn a lot of history when you were in school?
- What period of history do you find most interesting?
- Do you enjoy visiting museums or historical places?
- Is there a famous historical figure you admire?

### PART 2

> **SPEAKING CARD 9**
>
> **Describe an important historical event from your country or from another country that you know well.**
>
> *You should say*
> - What the event is
> - When and where it happened
> - What caused the event or what it was about
>
> *and explain why you think this event was important.*

### PART 3

- Why do you think people should learn about history?
- How can schools make history lessons more interesting for students?
- Do you think history should be a compulsory subject? Why or why not?
- How does learning history help people understand the present?
- What types of historical events are most important for people to learn?

## 10. UNIT 10

### PART 1

- What kinds of things make you happy?
- How do you usually express your feelings?
- Are you good at controlling your emotions?
- What do you do when you feel stressed or upset?

### PART 2

> **SPEAKING CARD 10**
>
> **Describe a time when you experienced positive emotions because of a surprise.**
>
> *You should say*
> - What the surprise was
> - When and where it happened
> - Who was involved
>
> *and explain why it made you feel positive.*

### PART 3

- Do you think people today express their emotions more openly than in the past? Why?
- How important is it for children to learn how to manage their emotions?
- Do you think emotional intelligence is more important than academic skills?
- How do cultural differences influence the way people express emotions?
- Should schools teach students about emotional wellbeing? Why or why not?
