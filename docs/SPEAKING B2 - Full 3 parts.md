# SPEAKING B2 — Full 3 Parts

## TOPIC 1: SHOPPING

### PART 1

1. Do you enjoy shopping?
2. How often do you go shopping?
3. Do you like shopping online?
4. What do you shop for?
5. Is it difficult for you to make a choice when you shop?
6. How do you choose your shopping place?
7. Do you prefer to go shopping in the shopping mall or on the street market?
8. When was the last time you went to a street market?
9. Do you prefer to go shopping alone?
10. What do you find annoying about shopping?
11. Do you compare prices when you shop?
12. What do you like to buy when you go shopping?
13. What is the most expensive item that you have purchased?

### PART 2 (Viết Outline + Ôn tập kỹ)

> **Describe your favorite shop that you often go to.**
>
> You should say
> - What sorts of product or service it sells
> - Where it is located
> - What the shop looks like
>
> And explain why you like going to it.

### PART 3

1. What risks would you take when shopping online? Why?
2. Will large shopping malls continue to be popular, despite the growth of internet shopping?
3. What kinds of things do people in your country often buy from online shops?
4. Why has online shopping become so popular in many countries?
5. What types of shops do teenagers like best in your country?
6. Do you agree that the prices of all goods should be lower on internet shopping sites than in shops?
7. Why do some people like shopping with friends?
8. How have shopping habits changed over recent years?
9. Do you think men and women have different opinions about shopping?
10. Is shopping a popular activity in your country?

## TOPIC 6: TECHNOLOGY

### PART 1

1. What kind of technological devices do you use most frequently?
2. Is there any new piece of technology you want to buy? Why that one?
3. Do you prefer using a smartphone, a laptop, or a tablet? Why?
4. Have you ever taught someone (like a family member) how to use a new gadget?
5. Do you think you spend too much or too little time with technology?
6. Do you have a favorite app or website you visit regularly? What is it?
7. What will you bring when you go out?
8. How would you remind yourself of something you need to bring?
9. What made you happy when you were little?
10. Do you feel happy when buying new things?
11. Is there anything that has made you feel happy lately?

### PART 2 (Viết Outline + Ôn tập kỹ)

> **Describe an object or invention that you use regularly.**
>
> You should say
> - What the object or invention is
> - What it looks like
> - Why and how often you use it
>
> And explain how you feel about it.

### PART 3

1. How do you think technology has changed the way people communicate with each other?
2. What are some potential drawbacks of relying too heavily on technological devices?
3. In your opinion, does technology make our lives easier or more complicated? Why?
4. How do you see the role of artificial intelligence shaping future inventions and daily life?
5. What impact do you think social media has on personal relationships and society as a whole?
6. Should schools prioritize teaching students how to use new technology effectively?
7. How might rapid technological advancements affect employment and job security?
8. Do you believe governments should regulate the use and development of certain technologies? In what ways?
9. How can people maintain a balance between embracing new technology and preserving traditional ways of life?
10. In the future, do you think technology will replace many human tasks, or will it create new opportunities?

## TOPIC 7: ENVIRONMENT

### PART 1

1. Do you care about environmental issues? Why or why not?
2. What do you usually do to help protect the environment in your daily life?
3. Is recycling common where you live? Why do you think that is?
4. Have you noticed any changes in weather patterns or local nature recently?
5. Do you prefer to travel using public transport or a private car? Why?
6. Have you ever participated in an environmental campaign or activity?
7. Do you think schools in your country do enough to educate children about the environment?
8. Are there any green spaces or parks near your home? How often do you visit them?
9. What type of environmental problem concerns you the most (e.g., air pollution, water pollution)?
10. Do you think technology can help solve environmental issues? In what ways?

### PART 2 (Viết Outline + Ôn tập kỹ)

> **Describe a serious environmental problem in your country.**
>
> You should say
> - What it is
> - What causes this problem
> - What the effects of this problem are
>
> And explain what can be done to solve this problem.

### PART 3

1. Why do you think environmental problems have become more serious in recent years?
2. What role should governments play in addressing environmental issues?
3. Do you believe stricter regulations can effectively reduce pollution? Why or why not?
4. How can companies be encouraged to adopt more eco-friendly practices?
5. To what extent do you think individual efforts, like recycling and reducing waste, can make a difference?
6. How might climate change affect different regions of the world differently?
7. Should wealthy countries bear more responsibility for environmental damage?
8. In what ways can technology help monitor and prevent environmental damage (e.g., drones, sensors)?
9. What are the challenges in trying to balance economic growth and environmental protection?
10. How can society shift toward a more sustainable lifestyle in the future?

## TOPIC 8: MEDICINE

### PART 1

1. Do you visit the doctor regularly for check-ups, or only when you're feeling unwell?
2. Have you ever felt anxious before going to see a medical professional?
3. Do you think it's important to have a good relationship with your doctor or dentist?
4. When was the last time you had a routine health screening?
5. Are you someone who follows health advice from doctors strictly, or do you make your own decisions?
6. What kinds of health or wellness apps do you use, if any?
7. Have you ever considered a career in healthcare? Why or why not?
8. Do people in your family usually go to the same doctor or do they each have their own?
9. How do you usually feel when you have to get a medical test or procedure done?
10. Do you think medical professionals in your country are generally appreciated?

### PART 2 (Viết Outline + Ôn tập kỹ)

> **Describe a time when you have a positive experience with a medical professional such as a doctor, a nurse or dentist.**
>
> You should say
> - Who the medical professional was
> - Why you had to see him or her
> - Why it was a positive experience for you
>
> And explain how you felt about the experience.

### PART 3

1. Why do you think a positive experience with a medical professional can be important for patients?
2. In your opinion, how can communication skills impact the doctor-patient relationship?
3. Do you believe that technology has improved the quality of healthcare services? In what ways?
4. Should governments invest more in preventative healthcare or in treatments for existing illnesses? Why?
5. How can medical professionals build trust with patients who are nervous or skeptical?
6. What are the advantages and disadvantages of telemedicine compared to in-person consultations?
7. Do you think there is a difference in patient care between private and public healthcare systems? Why or why not?
8. How important is it for medical professionals to continue their education throughout their careers?
9. What role do you think mental health services should play within the broader healthcare system?
10. How might cultural differences affect the way patients and medical professionals communicate and interact?
